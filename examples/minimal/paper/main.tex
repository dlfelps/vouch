\section{Results at \vouch{toy.centroid.acc.mean}}

(\vouch[.3f]{toy.centroid.acc.mean} as a fraction), while the majority baseline
reaches \vouch{toy.majority.acc}.

accuracy than the majority baseline}. Each model is evaluated on
\vouch{toy.centroid.n_test} test points for each of \vouch{train.param.seeds} seeds.
